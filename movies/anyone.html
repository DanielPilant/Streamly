Anyone But You is a 2023 romantic comedy film starring Sydney Sweeney and Glen Powell. The story follows Bea and Ben, two young people who had a terrible first date but are forced to pretend to be a couple during a destination wedding in Australia. What begins as mutual dislike slowly turns into unexpected romance.
