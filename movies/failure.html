Failure to Launch is a 2006 romantic comedy film starring Matthew McConaughey and Sarah Jessica Parker. The story follows Tripp, a 35-year-old man who still lives with his parents. His parents hire Paula, a professional motivator who helps men move out of their parents’ homes — but things get complicated when she unexpectedly falls in love with her client. The film explores themes of independence, love, and the difficulty of growing up.
